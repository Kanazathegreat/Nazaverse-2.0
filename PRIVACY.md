# Privacy Policy for Nazaverse

**Last Updated:** October 2026

Nazaverse ("the App") is an AI companion application that operates locally on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use the App.

## 1. Local Data Storage
The App is designed with a "local-first" privacy model. All of your personal data, including:
- API Keys (e.g., Google Gemini, OpenAI)
- Hermes Server Configurations
- Chat Histories and Sessions
- App Settings (Language, Pet configurations)

are stored **locally** on your device (via browser IndexedDB/LocalStorage or platform Secure Storage for native builds). We do not operate any backend servers to collect, store, or process this data.

## 2. API Keys and Third-Party Providers
To function, the App sends your text prompts and selected chat history directly to the AI provider you configure (such as Google or OpenAI) or your own local Hermes Agent. 
- The App **does not intercept** or proxy these requests through our servers.
- When using third-party providers, your data is subject to their respective Privacy Policies and Terms of Service.

## 3. Analytics and Telemetry
The App does not include built-in telemetry, crash reporting, or user tracking SDKs. We do not track your usage behavior.

## 4. In-App Purchases
For users purchasing cosmetic items (pets, skins) via Google Play Billing, the transaction and entitlement verification are handled entirely by Google Play Services. The App checks this entitlement directly against the Play Store app on your device. We do not receive or store your payment information.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please refer to the project repository.
